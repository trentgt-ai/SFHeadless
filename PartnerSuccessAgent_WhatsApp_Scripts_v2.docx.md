## Industry adaptation checklist

Before scripting a new industry, decide these seven things:

1\. WHO is the partner persona? (channel rep, franchisee, broker, VAR, dealer, ISV)

2\. WHAT SYSTEM does authentication unlock? (portal / DMS / PRM / dealer BMS / broker portal)

3\. WHAT DEAL OBJECT gets touched? (registered opportunity / vehicle hold / configured quote / RFQ / SOW)

4\. WHAT'S THE GROUNDED-INTEL MOMENT? (earnings-call quote, prior rep's CRM note, competitor mention, compliance flag)

5\. WHAT INCENTIVE is live, with a HARD expiry? (NPI bonus, hybrid rebate, SPIF, MDF, tier accelerator)

6\. WHAT'S THE REP'S OWN NUDGE? (quota %, tier progression, quarter target, GTM milestone)

7\. WHAT'S THE MULTI-UPDATE MOMENT? (test-drive log, site-visit notes, call outcome, install checklist)
